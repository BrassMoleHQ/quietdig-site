How it works

  1. SEC Form D filings. We read the new private-raise filings each week and keep operating companies, not funds.
  2. Live job boards. We look for senior engineering roles on each company's own careers board and list only roles that are live.
  3. Every fact re-checked. Each line carries its source and is re-checked before the issue goes out. A fact that fails the check is cut, not guessed.
  4. Quiet first. Companies with no news for their exact name lead the list; lightly covered ones follow.
